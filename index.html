THE PROGRAMME

One preparation system.
Different exam pathways.

CEP adds competitive-exam skills around regular school learning: concept strength, reasoning, speed, accuracy, exam temperament and repeated testing.

01

After school

Dedicated preparation beyond regular class hours, organised around student level and target exam.

02

English material

Worksheets progress from subject to chapter, topic and sub-topic.

03

School assessment

Regular school testing helps determine readiness and next steps.

04

Parent consent

External registration happens only for eligible students whose families opt in.

THE CORE RULE

Preparation is inclusive.
Registration is selective.

Students can develop competitive-exam ability even when they do not immediately sit an external test. That is why preparation and registration are deliberately separate decisions.
