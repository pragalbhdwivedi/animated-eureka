HOW CEP WORKS

Serious preparation beyond regular school hours.

CEP gives students structured practice for competitive examinations while keeping regular school learning at the centre. The programme builds concepts, reasoning, speed, accuracy and exam familiarity over time.

01

2–3 hours a day

Guided after-school preparation, with the actual timetable adjusted by class and upcoming examinations.

02

Seven-day model

Preparation continues across the week, with intensity changing according to school calendar and exam proximity.

03

English material

Structured worksheets progress from subject to chapter, topic and sub-topic.

04

Multiple test formats

Students practise OMR, computer-based and written formats where relevant.

STUDENT EXPERIENCE

Practice that becomes progressively exam-ready.

Students work through structured material, timed assessments, mock examinations, revision and doubt-clearing. The exact combination varies by class and target exam.

EXTERNAL REGISTRATION

Preparation does not automatically mean registration.

A student may benefit from CEP preparation without being entered into every external examination. Registration depends on the examination’s official eligibility rules, school assessment and parent/student consent.
