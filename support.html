FAQ

Costs and logistics

Is the official exam fee included in ₹1,500?

No. ₹1,500/month is the after-school preparation charge. Any official application or examination fee charged by the exam authority is communicated separately.

Are school mock tests charged separately?

School-conducted mock tests are provided by the school as part of the CEP support model.

Is transport always free?

The school plans transport support for centres within up to 250 km. The final arrangement for a particular exam is communicated before the examination because route, centre and student count can differ.

What happens for a centre beyond 250 km?

The school will communicate the practical arrangement and any distance-based cost before the exam.
