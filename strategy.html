OUR STRATEGY

Prepare broadly. Select fairly. Intensify intelligently.

The exam date changes the intensity, not the existence of preparation. Students build the base first, then move into increasingly exam-specific practice.

01

Map the opportunity

Match exam pathways to class, age, syllabus overlap, application window and likely centre requirements.

02

Build the foundation

Topic-wise and sub-topic-wise practice in core subjects, mental ability and language.

03

Run school screening

Class-level OMR assessments measure readiness, speed, accuracy and recurring error patterns.

04

Confirm eligibility + consent

Only eligible students whose families opt in are formally registered for the relevant external exam.

05

Increase exam intensity

Practice shifts toward target pattern, time pressure, full mocks, CBT/OMR and written formats where relevant.

06

Support exam day

Documents, logistics and transport support are planned around the announced examination centre.

07

Review and feed forward

Results and mock data return to the preparation cycle, improving the next student group.

SCREENING

Why we do not register everyone automatically

Every student prepares, but not every student registers?

Correct. The preparation itself has value. External registration is a separate decision based on eligibility, readiness and family consent.

How is readiness measured?

Class-level OMR assessments, mock tests, speed, accuracy, error patterns and consistency are used together rather than relying on a single score.

What happens as the exam approaches?

The programme becomes more exam-specific: full mocks, timing pressure, target question patterns and the required answer format receive greater weight.
