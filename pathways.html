BEFORE AN APPLICATION

Eligibility is checked for each examination.

Every external examination has its own age, class, date and documentation rules. The school checks the applicable official notification before registration and communicates the current requirements to families.

IMPORTANT

Current notifications always take precedence.

Are all exam names on this page offered every year?

No. They show the kinds of pathways CEP can support. Availability, dates, class eligibility and application rules depend on the official notification and the school’s current programme announcement.

Will the school publish current dates?

Yes. When an examination is opened for a class, families will receive the current application window, eligibility, required documents and other practical details.

Can a student prepare without applying?

Yes. CEP preparation can continue building reasoning, subject depth and test skills even when the student does not sit a particular external exam.
