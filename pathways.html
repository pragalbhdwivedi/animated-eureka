HOW WE ASSIGN A TARGET

Class first.
Notification second.
Readiness third.

Being interested in an exam is not enough. The school checks the student’s class, age, the official rules, syllabus fit, preparation window and practical centre requirements before a target becomes a registration plan.

01

Eligibility

Age, class and all other official conditions must be satisfied.

02

Preparation fit

The student should have enough time to prepare properly, not merely appear.

03

Readiness score

School OMR and mock performance help determine whether registration is sensible.

04

Consent

Parent and student consent is required before an external application is submitted.

IMPORTANT

Exam rules can change.

Are the exam names on this page guaranteed every year?

No. They are programme pathways, not a promise of annual registration. Availability, dates, class eligibility and rules depend on each examination authority.

Will the school publish current dates?

Yes. When a target exam is activated for a class, the school will communicate the current application window, eligibility, likely centre information and registration requirements.

Can a student prepare without applying?

Yes. Foundation preparation continues to build reasoning, subject depth and test skills even when the student does not sit that particular external exam.
